A note on the publication.

A few words about what this is, who is responsible, and the small choices that make it look the way it does.

The Publication

Stuff of Interest is published irregularly, in fits and starts, when there is something worth saying and time enough to say it well. There is no schedule, no newsletter, and no particular plan.

Posts arrive in three flavours: essays, which take a position; notebook entries, which are working things out; and diary entries, which are simply trying to remember.

The Setting

Display type is set in Fraunces, a soft-edged variable serif drawn by Phaedra Charles and Flavia Zimbardi. Body text is EB Garamond, Georg Duffner's open-source revival of Claude Garamont's sixteenth-century romans. Metadata and small caps use IBM Plex Mono.

Colours: a paper cream #f2e9d4, a warm ink #1c1812, and a single accent — a deep vermilion #a8331c — used sparingly, the way a good book is bound.

The Workshop

Hand-written HTML and CSS. No build step, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies. The page you are reading is a flat file on a static host — older than most of the web and likely to outlast it.

Hosted on GitHub Pages. Source available, should you be curious, on GitHub.

Correspondence

Reachable by the usual means. Thoughtful disagreement is welcomed, especially in the form of a letter.
